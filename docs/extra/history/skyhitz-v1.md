# Skyhitz v1 (archived)

Before HITZ Protocol, Skyhitz ran a music streaming and investing
platform on Stellar. Listeners paid small HITZ fees to stream, like and
download tracks, investors staked HITZ for equity in music entries, and a
Soroban core contract recorded those actions and distributed rewards. Its
HITZ was a classic Stellar asset with a maximum supply of 21M, still listed
as the legacy asset in [stellar.toml](https://skyhitz.io/.well-known/stellar.toml).

That platform is archived. Its code, and the documentation this site
published until November 2026, are kept in
[skyhitz/skyhitz](https://github.com/skyhitz/skyhitz)
([documentation source](https://github.com/skyhitz/skyhitz/tree/main/packages/docs/docs)).

Today's HITZ is a different token: the Soroban token of HITZ Protocol,
described in the [whitepaper](/whitepaper), with its supply capped at 100M,
trading on the [Sovereign Vault](/vault/integration). Holders of the legacy platform are
offered HITZ and XLM through the reparation campaign described in
[App and gateway](/app/frontend#legacy-reparation-campaign-one-time-run).
